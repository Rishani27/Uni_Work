Getting started

Set up your account, personalize your profile, and explore core features like calendar, jobs, and wallet.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your university email, verify your address, and log in to your dashboard.

2

Complete your profile

Add your name, contact number, skills, and a short bio. Aim for 100% completion to unlock recommendations.

3

Explore your dashboard

Use the calendar for events, check notifications, browse jobs, and track your wallet balance in LKR.

4

Apply and engage

Apply to roles, message coordinators, and keep your schedule updated to stay on top of deadlines.

FAQs

Go to the login page and select “Forgot password”. Enter your registered email to receive a reset link. If you don’t see the email, check spam or try again after a few minutes.
Ensure you’ve added your contact number, skills, education, and a profile photo. Some modules—like wallet setup or calendar preferences—also contribute to completion.
Open the Calendar tab and click a date to add an event. Use tags for classes, deadlines, or interviews. Event dots and highlights help you spot busy days quickly.
The wallet displays balances and transactions in LKR. You can export statements and filter by date or category.
Visit the Jobs tab, filter by role or location, and open a listing to review details. Click “Apply” and attach your resume or portfolio if required.

Contact support

Describe your issue clearly—include screenshots or steps to reproduce if possible. We’ll get back to you promptly.
